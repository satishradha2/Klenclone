## Known raw-export behavior

- Customer, supplier, purchase, sales, POS, return, and payment-account exports may append a presentation total/footer row.
- Product export appends a non-business row containing bulk-action UI text.
- The raw file remains unchanged; derived staging transforms will identify and quarantine these rows.
- Sales-detail month exports are intentionally supplemented by five half-day boundary exports. BizModo's time filter displayed `12:00 AM` through `11:59 PM` but omitted 274 boundary rows; the controlled union reconciles exactly to the annual UI count of 8,466.
- Primary cash-flow partitions are March, April, May, June, July, two August halves, and September: 4,470 rows with zero variance to the annual UI count. The five `cash_flow_boundary_*` files overlap those primary partitions and are retained only as diagnostic evidence; they must not be added to the staging union.
- Current scope covers visible 2026 transactional data. Read-only checks of the 2025 views returned no records, but only a database backup can prove that no older or hidden records exist.
- Stock-transfer detail evidence is a later live snapshot: 917 transfers and 4,219 product lines versus 916 headers in the earlier CSV.
- Secret and credential values are deliberately excluded from the business-settings evidence; field presence and configured state are retained.
