# Domestic customer credit and refund workflow — 2026-09-29

## Implemented code paths

1. A posted AED customer invoice is the immutable source for a physical return or a distinct pricing-only credit note.
2. Physical returns keep quantity disposition and original dispatch-cost controls. Pricing-only credits reference one original invoice line, capture the corrected effective net unit price, calculate net/VAT credit in AED cents, and have no stock movement. They use separate revision-checked maker/checker approval and cannot overlap a physical return on the same invoice SKU. One active pricing correction per invoice line prevents ambiguous overlapping unit claims.
3. Posted credits reduce open receivables first. The remainder is a customer-refund payable (2130). Submitted and approved refunds reserve the payable, preventing duplicate payout claims.
4. An approved refund must match an imported bank debit of the exact account, amount, and date. The statement itself requires independent reconciliation approval before payout posting. The journal debits 2130 and credits the bank GL, with fingerprinted idempotent posting.
5. A paid refund is immutable bank history. A separate compensating receipt can recover some or all of that payout. It has its own maker/checker approval, exact imported bank-credit match, independently approved reconciliation, and idempotent journal that debits bank and reopens 2130. A new payout may then settle the reopened liability.
6. Invoice settlement, customer statements, sales-return liability metrics, and integrated posting/reversal dependencies include these documents. The Sales Returns workspace exposes creation, decisions, bank matching, journal preview, and gated posting together.
7. The local preview's provisioned `operations_administrator` receives credit/refund creation and submission rights; its `independent_approver` receives approval and rehearsal rights. A browser check exposed and prompted this permission correction on 2026-09-30. Maker-checker still rejects self-approval.
